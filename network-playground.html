Lab 05 · Neural networks

Network Playground

A real neural network, trained from random knobs while you watch. The map shows what it currently believes about every spot on the plane. The wiring shows the knobs themselves. Nothing here is pre-computed: press Play and it learns, in your browser, by the same downhill rule as the straight line in lab 02.

Scope: a fully connected network with two inputs, one or two hidden layers of up to eight neurons, and one sigmoid output, trained by mini-batch gradient descent (16 examples a step) on cross-entropy. 160 examples train it and 40 are held out. Adam and other adaptive optimisers, dropout, normalisation layers and anything involving images or text are omitted. Different random starts give different runs; “New start” shows how much.

It starts as noise

At step zero the map is a meaningless smear, because the knobs are random. Every trained network you have ever used began like this.

Structure appears on its own

Watch the wiring: a few connections thicken and the rest stay thin. Nobody assigned those roles. They emerged because they lowered the loss.

Luck of the start is real

Press “New start” a few times on a small network. Some starts succeed and some stall. Larger networks are more forgiving, which is one reason people build them bigger than strictly necessary.
